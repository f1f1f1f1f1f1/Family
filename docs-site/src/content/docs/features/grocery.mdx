# Grocery

The **Lists** screen shows your shopping lists: Beacon's built-in **Shopping List**, and the Home Assistant to-do lists you shop from, such as [AnyList](https://www.anylist.com/), HA's own Shopping List, or Google Tasks. You can add items and check them off from the display.

---

## Where lists come from

- **Beacon's Shopping List** is built in and needs no setup. The Family server saves it, so every display shows the same list.
- **Home Assistant lists** are HA's to-do (`todo.*`) entities, except unavailable ones. Any integration that provides one works: Beacon adds and checks off items with HA's `todo.add_item` and `todo.update_item` actions.

These HA lists are shopping lists:

1. The ones ticked under **Settings > Integrations > Grocery > Grocery Lists**
2. If none are ticked, lists with a word in their name starting with grocer, shopping, costco, walmart, target, store, pantry, fridge, freezer, inventory, or meal (for example "Groceries" or "Costco run")

Every other HA list is on the **Tasks** screen instead, with Beacon's built-in To-Do list.

---

## Connecting AnyList

AnyList has no official public API, so it connects through a custom Home Assistant integration:

1. Install the [AnyList custom component](https://github.com/kevdliu/hacs-anylist) via HACS (Home Assistant Community Store), then restart Home Assistant
2. Go to **Settings > Devices & Services**, click **Add Integration**, and search for **AnyList**
3. Enter your AnyList email and password, then click **Submit**

The integration adds a to-do list for each of your AnyList lists. Other to-do integrations, such as HA's **Shopping List** or **Google Tasks**, are added the same way.

## Settings

In **Settings > Integrations > Grocery**:

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Grocery Lists** | Which HA lists are shopping lists (see above) |
| **Default Grocery List** | The list the Lists screen opens on. *Auto* opens the first one |
| **Shopping Card List** | The list shown by dashboard shopping cards that don't pick their own. *None* uses the default grocery list |
| **Hide Local Shopping List** | Leaves out Beacon's built-in Shopping List, showing only HA lists |

## Using the Lists screen

Click **Lists** (the shopping cart icon) in the sidebar. On a phone, tap **More**, then **Lists**.

- **Pick a list** from the menu at the top. Home Assistant lists are marked "(HA)".
- **Add an item**: type it in the box at the top and press Enter or tap **+**.
- **Check an item off** by tapping it, and tap it again to uncheck it. Checked items move to **Completed**, which you can tap to collapse.
- **Delete an item** from Beacon's built-in list with its trash icon. Delete items from an HA list in the app it belongs to.

Changes show straight away. If Home Assistant doesn't take one, a checked item goes back to how it was, and an added item stays in the box.

Home Assistant lists reload every 30 seconds while the display is awake, and shortly after each change made here, so items added in AnyList or another app appear by themselves.

## Troubleshooting

### A list is missing

1. In Home Assistant, check that its `todo.*` entity exists and isn't unavailable in **Developer Tools > States**
2. If you ticked any **Grocery Lists**, tick this one too. If you haven't, it may be on the **Tasks** screen, because its name doesn't sound like a shopping list
3. For Beacon's own Shopping List, check that **Hide Local Shopping List** is off

### "No lists available"

No list counts as a shopping list: **Hide Local Shopping List** is on, and Home Assistant has no shopping list or isn't connected.

### Added or checked items don't stick

1. Check that the list's integration is working in **Developer Tools > States**
2. Try the same change with the `todo.add_item` or `todo.update_item` action in **Developer Tools > Actions**
3. Open the browser console (F12) and look for "GroceryView: Failed to…" messages
